Privacy Policy for Zmanim

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Contact: zmanim.apps@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes what information Zmanim uses, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

In short: Zmanim uses your location to calculate zmanim and to time its notifications. It keeps that location on your device. The developers of Zmanim never receive it. To give a location a name, your coordinates (or the place you type) are sent to your device's geocoding service, which on most Android devices is provided by Google. Zmanim collects no usage, analytics or crash data, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data.

If you have ANY questions, please do not hesitate to email zmanim.apps@gmail.com (Please note that doing so will reveal your email address. But...you probably knew that already.)

The Information Zmanim Uses

Your location

If you choose Use My Location and allow it, Zmanim gets your device's location from Android, through Google Play services. It does this while the app is running and, if you allow access "All the time", also in the background, when the app is closed or not in use. The location includes your latitude, longitude and, when the device provides it, altitude.

Instead of sharing your location, you can type a place, or a latitude and longitude, in Zmanim's location settings.

What Zmanim does not collect

Zmanim collects no usage data, analytics or crash reports, and contains no advertising.

How Your Location Is Used

  • Display of Zmanim: If, and only if, you have provided consent to use your location, this location is used to calculate the zmanim for the day(s) you choose to view.
  • Notifications: If, and only if, you have allowed location access "All the time", your location is checked in the background so that notifications for upcoming zmanim come at the right times for where you are.
  • Naming the place: To show the name of your location (for example, "Albany, New York"), or to find a place you type, Zmanim asks your device's geocoding service, which is part of Android. That service sends the coordinates, or the text you typed, to its provider. On most Android devices the provider is Google, and its privacy policy covers what it does with them.

Sharing of Personal Data

The developers of Zmanim do not receive your location or any other personal information, and do not sell or share it with anyone. The only place your location goes outside your device is the geocoding lookup described above.

Retention of Personal Data

  • Location Data: Zmanim keeps your most recent location, and the names of places it has looked up, on your device so that it can avoid repeating lookups. They stay until you uninstall Zmanim or clear its storage.
  • Email: If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. In such a case, a prominent notice will be displayed in Zmanim, prior to the change becoming effective and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
