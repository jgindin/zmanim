You currently have Zmanim configured to use your current location for calculating the zmanim.

In order to display timely and accurate notifications of upcoming zmanim, you must allow Zmanim to acquire your location even when the app is not in use (in the background).

In the next dialog, you will have a choice about allowing Zmanim to acquire your location.

  • Choosing to allow access "All the time" is the only way for Zmanim to calculate when notifications are to be displayed.
  • Selecting any other option will make notifications unreliable at best. You will still have full use of all functionality while you're interacting with the Zmanim application. This includes allowing Zmanim to find your location.
If you wish to receive notifications, but do not want Zmanim to obtain your location in the background, then it is suggested that you switch to specifying the location yourself. This can be done by:
  1. Tap the 3 vertical dots on the main screen;
  2. Tap 'Location'
  3. Change the selection to either "Use Specified Place" or "Use Specified Lattitude & Longitude"
