Changing Zmanim

  • Click on the zman you want to change
  • In the resulting dialog, click on the desired calculation
  • The dialog will go away, and that choice will be remembered every time you run Zmanim.

Changing Dates

  • Swipe right-to-left to move to the previous day; swipe left-to-right to move to the next day.
  • Click on the secular date in the upper left-hand corner to get a date selector.

Changing Locations

Click the 'menu' button and choose Location. Options include:
  • "Use My Location" to have Zmanim do its best to use your current location. Does not require internet connectivity.
  • "Use Specified Place" to enter a particular location. Requires internet connectivity to lookup the actual location.
  • "Use Specified Latitude & Longitude" to use a particular location, but without requiring any network connectivity.
You can also select a particular time zone to use, if you want to see the times other than the time zone of your device.
