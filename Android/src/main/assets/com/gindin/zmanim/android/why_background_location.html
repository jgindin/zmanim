This app can find your location in order to display zmanim for your current location, and may do so even when the application is closed or not in use in order to display timely and accurate notifications of upcoming zmanim.

In the next dialog, you will have a choice about allowing Zmanim to acquire your location. This requires you to choose to allow access "All the time".

If you wish to receive notifications, but do not want Zmanim to obtain your location in the background, then it is suggested that you switch to specifying the location yourself. This can be done by:

  1. Tap the 3 vertical dots on the main screen;
  2. Tap 'Location'
  3. Change the selection to either "Use Specified Place" or "Use Specified Lattitude & Longitude"
