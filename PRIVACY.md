# Privacy Policy for Zmanim for Android

Zmanim for Android does not store ANY personally identifiable data.

If you have ANY questions, please do not hesitate to email: zmanim.apps@gmail.com. (Please note 
that doing so will reveal your email address. But...you probably knew that already.)


## Crash Reports
The only data which ever leaves your phone may be a crash report (and, honestly, let's hope that 
never happens). 

The crash report will include location information, but there is ABSOLUTELY no way for that 
location to be traced back or in any way identified with you. 

The crash report will also include some basic data about your device. Again, there is NO WAY for
this information to be used to identify you.


## User Submitted Feedback
In case you choose to share feedback through the Zmanim for Android application with the developers 
of Zmanim for Android, then you may also choose to include an email address. Or not. It's up to 
you. The information sent with the feedback is the same as what is included with a crash report,
plus any additional message you may (or may not!) choose to include.


## Permissions
Zmanim for Android does give you the _option_ of allowing the application to determine your current
location. This location is ONLY used to display the correct zmanim for you in your current location.
Except as noted above (see Crash Reports and User Submitted Feedback), this location data does not
leave your device.
