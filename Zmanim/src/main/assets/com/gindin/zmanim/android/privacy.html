Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Information Collected while Using the Application

While using Zmanim, we may collect (with your prior consent) your current location. This can happen while the app is running or, (again, with your prior consent), even when the app is in the background (which includes when the app is closed or not otherwise in use).

Usage Data

Usage Data may be collected automatically when using Zmanim.

When you use Zmanim we may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device You use, the IP address of Your mobile device, Your mobile operating system, location data, application configuration settings, and other diagnostic data.

Use of Your Personal Data

Zmanim may use Personal Data for the following purposes:
  • Display of Zmanim: If, and only if, you have provided consent to use your location, this location will be used to determine the zmanim for the day(s) you choose to view.
  • Notifications: If, and only if, you have provided consent to use your location, this location will be used to determine and present system notifications for upcoming zmanim.
  • Usage Data: In case of a crash, the Usage Data may be utilized to help track down and fix the problem.

Sharing of Personal Data

We will not share your personal information with anyone EVER.

Retention of Personal Data

  • Location Data: Location data (except when it is included in the Usage Data) never leaves your device and is only retained for the purposes described above.
  • Usage Data: IF any Usage Data is sent to the Developers of Zmanim (generally only when a crash is detected), your Personal Data will be retained ONLY for internal analysis purposes. This data is removed as soon as the analysis is complete.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. In such a case, a prominent notice will be displayed in Zmanim, prior to the change becoming effective and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
