Privacy Policy for Zmanim for Android

Zmanim for Android does not store ANY personally identifiable data.

If you have ANY questions, please do not hesitate to email zmanim.apps@gmail.com (Please note that doing so will reveal your email address. But...you probably knew that already.)

Permissions

Zmanim for Android does give you the option of allowing the application to determine your current location. This location is ONLY used to display the correct zmanim for you in your current location. Except as noted below (see Crash Reports and User Submitted Feedback), this location data does not leave your device.

Zmanim for Android also gives you the option of allowing the application to get your location in the background, even when it is closed or not in use. This capability is provided to allow you to have timely and accurate notifications based on your current location.

You may choose not to allow this. Instead, you can manually enter your location.

  • If you enter a location name, a network call will be made to resolve this location to a latitude and longitude. Without the latitude and longitude, there is no way to determine the zmanim.
  • If you enter a latitude and longitude, a network call will be made to determine a name (such as the city and state) corresponding to this location.

Your location data will NEVER be shared with any third party.

Crash Reports

The only data which ever leaves your phone may be a crash report (and, honestly, let's hope that never happens).

The crash report does not contain any information which is personally identifiable or in any way related to you; it simply contains data which will (hopefully) allow us to track down the bug and get it fixed.

The crash report may include location information. As noted before, there is ABSOLUTELY no way for that location to be traced back or in any way identified with you. If the location data is included in a crash report, that data will be deleted as soon as the crash report is no longer necessary to help diagnose the bug.

The crash report will also include some basic data about your device. Again, there is NO WAY for this information to be used to identify you. This data will NEVER be shared with a third party, and will be deleted as soon as the crash report is no longer necessary to help diagnose the bug.

Neither the crash report nor any data it contains will EVER be shared with any third party.

User Submitted Feedback

In case you choose to share feedback through the Zmanim for Android application with the developers of Zmanim for Android, then you may also choose to include an email address. Or not. It's up to you. The information sent with the feedback is the same as what is included with a crash report, plus any additional message you may (or may not!) choose to include.

As with crash reports, all data will be deleted as soon as the relevant feedback has been registered.
