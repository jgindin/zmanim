To show the most accurate zmanim according to where you are, allow Zmanim to access your location.

This setting only affects Zmanim for Android's ability to get your location while the app is running. (Only iIf this permission is granted will a subsequent explanation and request be made regarding access to obtaining your location in the background in order to display timely and accurate notifications.)

If you decline, you will be taken to a screen where you can instead manually specify a location or a latitude and longitude.
