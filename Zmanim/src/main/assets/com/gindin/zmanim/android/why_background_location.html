To show timely and accurate notifications of upcoming zmanim based on where you are, Zmanim for Android will need to access your location even when the app is closed or not in use (aka in the background).

This capability requires you to choose to allow access "All the time" on the next screen, and supersedes the permission previously granted to access your location while the app is running.

If you decline, you will be taken to a screen where you can instead manually specify a location or a latitude and longitude.
