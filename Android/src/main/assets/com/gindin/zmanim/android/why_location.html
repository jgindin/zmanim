To show the zmanim according to where you are, allow Zmanim to access your location. Zmanim will use your location to display the most accurate zmanim possible. If you prefer, you may specify the location yourself. This can be done by:
  1. Tap the 3 vertical dots on the main screen;
  2. Tap 'Location'
  3. Change the selection to either "Use Specified Place" or "Use Specified Lattitude & Longitude"
